Drop your audio files here (mp3 works best everywhere).

background.mp3  -> looping background music
blow.mp3        -> plays when the candles go out
unwrap.mp3      -> plays when a chocolate is unwrapped
open.mp3        -> plays when the envelope / gift opens
celebrate.mp3   -> plays during confetti/fireworks moments

All of these are optional. If a file is missing, that sound effect
is silently skipped — the site works perfectly without any music at
all, and the mute/unmute button just won't have anything to play.

Good royalty-free options if you need music: pixabay.com/music,
freesound.org, or YouTube's Audio Library.
